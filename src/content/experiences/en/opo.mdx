Technical and functional lead on a gamified compliance training platform. 3M+ users, 300+ companies, 30+ languages.

- Evolved the training catalogue in **React** (**data-driven** through a **Headless CMS**, **GraphQL**) and drove a **progressive migration to Svelte**.
- Built bespoke training modules to client requirements, delivered as **SCORM** packages or through the internal **SaaS**.
- Business logic in **Rescript**: **hexagonal architecture**, framework-agnostic, shared across projects.
- Maintained and evolved the internal **SaaS** (**Go**, **Vue.js**, **PostgreSQL**): progress tracking and score reporting.
- **Migrated the marketing site to Svelte** (Elder.js), **Islands** architecture, **SEO**-optimised. Elder.js maintainer.
- CI/CD (**Jest**, **Cypress**), run and monitoring (**Sentry**).
