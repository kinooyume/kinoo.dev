Ekeynox is a **typing** game, a way to practise typing faster and without mistakes.

It offers two modes, a **word race** and a **time trial**, and several kinds of content: **random words**, **quotes** or a **custom text**.

At the end of a run, players can review their **statistics** and **replay** in ghost mode against their previous score.
