Personal portfolio site built with **Astro** and **SolidJS**, tuned for performance and accessibility.

Smooth animations with **Anime.js** and a modular design system with **Design System** components.

**Islands** architecture for optimal loading, a validated contact form, and automated CI/CD with **conventional commits** and deployment on **Netlify**.
